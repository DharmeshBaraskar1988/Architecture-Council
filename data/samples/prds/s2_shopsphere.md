# ShopSphere marketplace

Sample product document S2 for testing the council. Fictional company, written for this project.

<!-- page: 1 -->
## 1. Overview

ShopSphere is an online marketplace where independent sellers list products and shoppers buy them.
We expect about 50,000 daily users at launch.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## 2. Team and platform

The engineering team has six developers.
The company already uses Azure for identity and email.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## 3. Features

- Browse and search the product catalog.
- Cart, wishlist and checkout.
- Shoppers pay by card at checkout.
- Order tracking and notifications by email and SMS.
- Seller onboarding and product listings.
- Admin console with an audit log.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## 4. Users, data and constraints

- Shoppers and sellers are in India.
- We store each shopper's name, address and phone number.
- The storefront is a public website.
- Services run in containers and are deployed through CI/CD.
- The production cloud budget is ₹1,50,000 per month.
